Who we are

OUR MISSION

Peppers #19044 is the student robotics team of Liceul Teoretic de Informatică „Grigore Moisil” in Iași. The team builds robots for FIRST Tech Challenge and brings STEM learning to its community through hands-on education, outreach and mentoring.

We're built on the idea that robotics is a team sport: mechanical, electrical, software and outreach working the same problem from every angle, under the same deadline.

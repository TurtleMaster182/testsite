Who we are

Engineering under pressure, on purpose.

Peppers Robotics is a student-run FIRST Tech Challenge team based in Iași. Every season we design, machine, wire and code a competition robot from a blank sheet of paper — then defend it on the field against the best teams in the region.

We're built on the idea that robotics is a team sport: mechanical, electrical, software and outreach working the same problem from every angle, under the same deadline.
